Behind the Pearl

Ocean to atelier, in six careful steps.

We work directly with third-generation pearl farms across the Ambon Sea and Kessennuma Bay, selecting only the top 3% of each harvest for lustre, surface, and shape. Every pearl is then hand-matched and set in our Bali atelier by goldsmiths who have shaped Siera pieces for over a decade.
